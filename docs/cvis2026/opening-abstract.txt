We study how to recover compact dynamical equations from noisy measurements using fewer expensive fitting steps. Profiled Joint Subset Search (PJSS) estimates the trajectory and equation together, screens alternative combinations of terms with a cheap approximation, and refits only a shortlist. The screen lets the trajectory adjust when the equation changes. On 80 paired tests, PJSS identifies the correct terms in 66 cases versus 61 for DEDi with complexity-based selection, at median runtimes of 14.4 and 5,930 seconds under the reported CPU/GPU setups. An operation-count study finds about 49 times fewer joint fits. Across 77 simulated systems, PJSS recovers the correct terms in 89.0% of 6,160 tests. Reliable use on measured data remains open.
