CVIS 2026: published requirements and this extended abstract's structure
Checked November 4, 2026.

OFFICIAL REQUIREMENTS VERIFIED ON THE CURRENT PUBLIC PAGES
https://cvis-conference.com/author-guidelines
- At most four pages of content, including figures and tables.
- Additional pages containing only cited references are allowed.
- Use the CVIS template and anonymize the submission.
- Every author needs an activated, updated OpenReview profile by the full deadline.

https://cvis-conference.com/reviewer-guidelines
- Primary criteria: technical correctness, clarity, and sound experiments/evaluation.
- State reasonable assumptions and interpret results correctly.
- Make the main ideas understandable to readers outside the exact topic.
- Preliminary results can be sufficient if they support the conclusions.
- Novelty and impact are developmental feedback areas, not rejection criteria by themselves.

WHAT IS NOT VERIFIED
The accessible current pages do not specify a mandatory list of abstract headings
or a word limit for the opening abstract paragraph. The current linked Overleaf
project failed to load, so additional template instructions remain unverified.
Do not substitute the older CVIS 2024 template's rules for the 2026 requirements.
Four pages is a maximum, not a mandatory length; this draft uses four content pages plus one page containing only cited
references, following the later request for fuller methods and a bibliography.

EDITORIAL STRUCTURE CHOSEN TO MATCH THE PROVIDED UAV REFERENCE
These are writing choices, not an invented official CVIS checklist:
The revised reference supplied November 5 guides the presentation: architecture
beneath the title, a column-width abstract, conventional numbered sections,
captions above tables and two-column cited references.
1. Introduction: problem, related approaches and the computational bottleneck.
2. Method: system overview, joint fitting, profiling, search and implementation.
3. Experimental setup: inputs, metrics, paired samples and baseline settings.
4. Results and discussion: recovery, time, ablations, forecasts, attractors, limits.
5. Conclusion: computational benefit, conditional accuracy and open measured-data use.
The architecture includes an actual recorded input and discovered Lorenz equations.
The short opening abstract states the task, method, main results and scope.
The narrative explains each component's purpose before its technical details.
The paper retains the core objective, profiled-score definition, search budget,
selection criterion and paired evaluation design. REPRODUCE.txt supplies the
full matrix formulas, numerical calibration, baseline grids and seeds, alongside
the source code and historical-replay limitations.
