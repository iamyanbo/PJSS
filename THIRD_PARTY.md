# Third-party material

The optional ODR-BINDy Python port in benchmarks/odr_bindy.py derives from Lloyd Fung's MIT-licensed ODR-BINDy implementation. Its upstream notice is preserved in [third_party/licenses/ODR-BINDy.txt](third_party/licenses/ODR-BINDy.txt). Source: https://github.com/llfung/ODR-BINDy .

The local research workspace also contains a line-by-line WSINDy_ODE port. That file, benchmarks/wsindy_port.py, is intentionally NOT redistributed here: the upstream README says All Rights Reserved and no redistribution license was found. Source: https://github.com/MathBioCU/WSINDy_ODE . The original local file was preserved. Optional WSINDy benchmark commands that import it require separate permission and are not runnable from this snapshot alone. Historical numeric comparisons remain labeled as recorded results.

DEDi, PySINDy, ARGOS, ODEFormer, PySR, dysts and optional solvers are separate dependencies, not bundled vendor source. Users must obtain these under their respective terms. Downloaded measured-data archives, third-party papers and local virtual environments are not included.

No new open-source license has been assigned to original PJSS materials in this release. Public visibility is not a blanket reuse license; third-party materials retain their own terms.
